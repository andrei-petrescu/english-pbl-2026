Support directory

The right starting point can depend on your ICHB campus. These published routes are listed separately so you can find the one that applies to you.

ICHB · Pallady

Counselling & wellbeing

ICHB Pallady's published wellbeing information says students can speak with their form teacher or a trusted teacher, or ask to be directed to the person responsible for school counselling.

Read ICHB Pallady's counselling and wellbeing information

To ask how to access the service, the school page links to an information request through admissions@ichb.ro . This is a general information route, not a private counselling channel. Do not include personal or medical details in an email.

General Pallady school contact information

ICHB · Colentina

Educational counselling

ICHB Colentina's published counselling page describes individual and group educational counselling for students. For the current way to request a conversation, contact the campus and ask to be directed to the counselling service.

Read ICHB Colentina's counselling information

The school's public contact page lists 021 204 04 44 and info.colentina@ichb.ro as general contacts. They are not listed as a private counselling channel; do not send sensitive details there.

General Colentina school contact information

Romania · Children and adolescents

Telefonul Copilului · 116 111

Telefonul Copilului describes 116 111 as a free national support line for children and adolescents. Check its official site for current hours and contact methods.

Call 116 111 · Check official service information

If there is immediate danger

Call 112 in Romania and tell a trusted adult nearby. The European Commission identifies 112 as Romania's emergency number.

European Commission: 112 emergency number

Campus services and contact details can change. Confirm the current route with your school. For a different ICHB campus, start at the official ICHB website.
