A guide to student emotional well-being

School, friendships, family, and change can bring complicated feelings. Find clear information, trusted resources, and ways to ask for support — at your own pace.

This student project is an educational guide, not a diagnosis or a substitute for a qualified professional.

Where would you like to begin?

There is no right order. Start with the question that feels most useful today.

  1. Understand what you might be feeling Common pressures, without labels or judgment
  2. Explore trusted resources Evidence-based guides from health organizations
  3. Find a person or place to ask ICHB campus support and help in Romania
  4. Try the needs-and-impact survey A demonstration only — answers are not collected

Need urgent help?

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 112 in Romania and tell a trusted adult nearby.
