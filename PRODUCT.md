# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript; no deployment target was specified.

## Users

Teenage students at ICHB in Romania who want approachable information about emotional well-being, ways to find support, and a view of the project's survey.

## Product Purpose

A school-project digital guide that helps students explore emotional well-being resources and school support. Its success is a safe, accessible website prototype covering evidence-based resources, a school support directory, and a needs-and-impact survey demonstration.

## Positioning

A student-focused educational prototype, not a clinical or emergency-care service. The survey demonstrates questions only; it does not collect real responses.

## Operating Context

The site is a tangible outcome of a school project. The supplied brief is in English; a different language or translation was not specified.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The user selected a multi-page resource hub and plain static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
- The survey must not transmit or retain responses.
- Support and health information must stay source-attributed and be rechecked before publication; service details and availability can change.
- No deployment target or approved live survey channel was specified.

## Evidence on Hand

The prototype links to these published sources:

- WHO, [Mental health of adolescents](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/adolescent-mental-health).
- WHO, [Doing What Matters in Times of Stress](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240003927).
- ICHB Pallady, [Counselling & wellbeing](https://pallady.ichb.ro/consiliere-wellbeing/) and [contact information](https://pallady.ichb.ro/contact/).
- ICHB Colentina, [Educational counselling](https://colentina.ichb.ro/consilier/) and [contact information](https://colentina.ichb.ro/contact/).
- Telefonul Copilului, [116 111](https://116111.ro/).
- European Commission, [112 emergency number](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/112).

School contact routes are identified as general information channels, not private counselling. No specialist approval or school endorsement of this prototype is implied.

## Product Principles

- Make information approachable and nonjudgmental for teenagers.
- Be clear about the limits of educational information and the survey prototype.
- Prefer verified support routes and transparent attribution over unsupported claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The requested resource must be accessible. Use semantic structure and ensure the pages and interactions work with keyboard navigation and assistive technology.
