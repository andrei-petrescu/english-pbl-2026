## Audience and job

ICHB students in Romania use this English-language prototype to understand emotional well-being topics, find trustworthy resources and school support, and preview the project survey.

## Content and constraints

Use the supplied project brief, source-attributed WHO information, official ICHB campus information, and verified Romanian support routes. The site is educational, not clinical or emergency care. Do not invent contacts or imply the survey collects responses. Preserve ordinary web navigation and a calm, readable column.
