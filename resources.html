These links come from the World Health Organization and official Romanian support services. Use them to learn more, not to diagnose yourself or someone else.

Start with the big picture

The WHO fact sheet introduces adolescent mental health, describes factors that can shape well-being, and explains why supportive environments matter. It also discusses when professional care may be needed.

Read “Mental health of adolescents” at the World Health Organization

Explore a practical stress guide

WHO describes Doing What Matters in Times of Stress as an illustrated, evidence-informed guide with practical skills for coping with stress. The publication page lists English and Romanian versions.

Open the WHO stress guide and choose a language

For a conversation or someone to talk to

Telefonul Copilului describes its 116 111 service as support for children and adolescents in Romania. Check the official site for current hours, contact options, and availability before relying on the service.

Visit Telefonul Copilului — 116 111 (Romanian)
